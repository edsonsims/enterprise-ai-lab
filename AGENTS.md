# Working in this study repository

Build a balanced portfolio of applied AI experiments and senior software engineering.
Use ROADMAP.md for scope and docs/decision-log.md for decisions. Preserve human edits.

- Prefer Python and TypeScript/Node.js for new work. Choose another language when the study benefits from it; do not make C# the focus without a new owner request.
- Keep all dates accurate. Never backdate, create empty activity, rewrite history to fill a graph, or manufacture engagement.
- Finish a useful, runnable milestone before marking it complete. Favor one coherent system and reusable modules over many unfinished repositories.
- For experiments, record the question, baseline, data provenance, method, raw results, reproduction command, and limitations. Synthetic data must always be labeled.
- Never imply a lab result is customer validation, production deployment, compliance certification, novel research, or measured business ROI.
- AI assistance is permitted in this repository. Keep AI_ASSISTANCE.md accurate. Automated review does not count as the owner's study or human review.
- Runtime examples must work offline by default. No secrets, employer code, customer data, paid APIs, infrastructure creation, package publishing, or live system changes without specific authorization.
- Run the documented test suite and reproduce the changed study before committing. Avoid unrelated refactors.
- Upstream contributions must address a real need, honor the repository's current AI and contribution policy, check for duplicate work, and include a reproduced failure and relevant tests. If human review is required, prepare the patch and stop before submission.
- Broaden meaningful contributions across repositories. Keep at most six outstanding portfolio upstream PRs, and at most one per upstream repository. Prioritize maintainer feedback over new PRs. Do not send nudges or unsolicited outreach.
- Keep pending PRs out of the public profile. Restore a selected contributions section only after at least three substantive code contributions have been merged across at least three upstream repositories, verified live. Feature accepted work and its practical impact. Notify the owner about accepted contributions or required action; do not promote submission counts or routine pending-PR updates.
- Distinguish prepared, submitted, merged, released, and personally studied. Do not claim acceptance until an upstream merge is verified.

- The connected RAG resource derives identity only from the dedicated verified JWT. Preserve mandatory tenant AND role filtering before vector top-k, offline model hash checks, pending-mutation fail-closed behavior, explicit generator failures and evidence provenance. Keep real model tests separate from controlled vectors/doubles.
